Services

Cleaning that holds up day after day.

Every building is different. We build the task list and schedule around yours, then hold to it.

Recurring janitorial

Nightly, weekly, or set-schedule cleaning of offices, work areas, and common spaces — dusting, waste, surfaces, and floors.

Restrooms & breakrooms

Full restroom and breakroom cleaning, sanitizing, and restocking, with supply levels reported so you are never caught short.

Lobbies & public areas

Entrances, reception, corridors, and meeting rooms opened or closed to a presentation standard your visitors notice.

Floors & interior glass

Routine floor care, entry glass, high-dusting, vents, and fixture detail on a periodic schedule alongside your regular service.

Turnover & move-out

One-time deep cleaning between tenants, after a renovation, or before a space is handed back — priced to a defined checklist.

Event resets

Before-and-after cleaning for meetings, community events, and public gatherings, scheduled around your calendar.

How we work

No surprises, on either side.

You know exactly what is being cleaned, how often, and who to call. So do we.

1

We walk your building

We look at the actual space — square footage, surfaces, traffic, access hours, and the problem spots you are tired of dealing with.

2

You get it in writing

A written scope listing every task, how often it happens, what is not included, and the price. Nothing vague, nothing assumed.

3

We show up and check it

An owner inspects the work against that scope, and you have one name and number for anything that needs fixing.
