Who we are built to serve

Four buyers, four different ways of placing the work.

Golden Crest is not competing for consumer cleaning leads. Each lane below is a defined procurement path with its own registration, notification, and award mechanics — and each one is reachable by a small vendor without an advertising budget.

01

County & municipal facilities

Administrative buildings, community centers, parks facilities, and public offices in Gaston County and surrounding towns that buy custodial service by facility or by route.

Vendor registration → bid notification → quote or ITB

02

Schools, colleges & housing authorities

Public school districts, community colleges, and housing authorities that award custodial work at the building level and often carry small-business participation goals.

Vendor portal → term contract or single-facility award

03

Prime contractors & facility managers

Established janitorial and facility-management primes holding regional or multi-site contracts who need local coverage, fill-in crews, or small-business subcontractors to meet subcontracting plan commitments.

Supplier outreach → subcontract or purchase order

04

Institutional & commercial accounts

Nonprofits, professional and administrative offices, and property managers who buy recurring janitorial directly and value a documented scope over the lowest bid.

Walkthrough → written scope → service agreement

Capabilities

What we perform — and what we will not bid.

A scope a vendor refuses is as useful to a buyer as one it accepts. Nothing moves from the right column to the left until the training, products, procedures, insurance, and staffing behind it exist.

Performed at launch In scope

  • Recurring janitorial service for office and administrative facilitiesFloors, restrooms, breakrooms, common areas, glass, waste, and high-touch surfaces on a written schedule.
  • Public-area opening and closing readinessLobbies, reception, corridors, meeting rooms, and public restrooms brought to a defined presentation standard.
  • Turnover, move-out, and event reset cleaningFixed-scope one-time work with a defined task list and completion check.
  • Periodic detail work within capabilityInterior glass, high-dusting, vent and fixture detail, and scheduled deep-clean cycles quoted separately from base scope.
  • Consumable monitoring and reorder reportingRestroom and breakroom supply levels reported to the facility contact; supply purchasing optional by agreement.
  • Documented completion checks and issue escalationWritten service record per visit and a named contact for corrective action.

Not offered until built Out of scope

  • Medical, clinical, or infection-control sanitationRequires exposure-control planning, product training, and insurance we have not established.
  • Hard-floor restoration, stripping, waxing, and carpet extractionEquipment-dependent. Added, or subcontracted transparently, only when the capability is real.
  • Exterior, high-access, or above-ground-level window workFall protection and separate coverage required.
  • Cleared or background-adjudicated personnelScreening will follow a written hiring policy. We will never describe screening as a security clearance.
  • Bonding or guaranteed response-time service levelsBonding and response commitments are priced per contract, not advertised.
  • Regional multi-site contractsBeyond current staffing. We would rather decline than fail a performance requirement.

Scope, frequencies, exclusions, access rules, and supply responsibility are defined in writing for every facility before pricing. Cleaning and disinfecting are quoted, staffed, and documented as separate activities.

Performance standard

How the work is specified, checked, and corrected.

Small custodial awards are lost on inconsistency, not on price. Each commitment below produces a document a facility contact or contracting officer can hold us to.

01

Written performance scope

Every facility gets a task list by area and frequency, with exclusions, access rules, service windows, and supply responsibility stated before a price is quoted.

Deliverable → Facility scope document

02

Consistent routine

Opening and closing procedures, zone assignments, and chemical and PPE handling are documented so the work does not change when the person does.

Deliverable → Site procedure sheet

03

Inspection cadence

A managing member inspects against the scope document on a stated schedule during startup and at an agreed interval afterward. The cadence is written into the agreement, not promised in general terms.

Deliverable → Signed inspection record

04

Issue ownership

One named contact receives deficiencies, with a response window agreed per contract and corrective action recorded and closed in writing.

Deliverable → Corrective action log

How to buy

Ways a buyer can place custodial work with us.

These are the mechanics, not a claim that every one of them is open to us today. The vendor status table below shows exactly which paths are available now and which are waiting on a registration.

Available now Direct commercial and institutional agreements, prime-contractor supplier conversations, and quote requests for scoped one-time work. We can meet, walk a facility, and put a written scope and price in front of you before the entity paperwork clears.
Available after registration Local and state vendor systems, federal solicitations, and any award requiring an active SAM registration, UEI, or certification. We will not respond to a solicitation we are not eligible to be awarded.
Not claimed Government purchase card acceptance is not established. No GSA schedule, IDIQ, or BPA is held. Any set-aside eligibility is unconfirmed until SBA issues a determination.

Micro-purchase

Federal: generally $15,000

Small scoped work a buyer can place with a qualified supplier without competitive quotations when the price is found reasonable. Lower statutory thresholds apply to some categories — including $2,500 for services covered by Service Contract Labor Standards, which recurring janitorial labor often is. State and local buyers set their own informal limits.

Simplified acquisition & informal quotes

Federal: up to $350,000

Quote-based awards for single facilities and defined routes. Small-business consideration commonly applies in this range, but the contracting officer still applies market research, responsibility, competition, and the specific set-aside stated in the solicitation.

Subcontract under a prime

No registration required

The fastest realistic path for a new vendor. Primes holding regional custodial or facility-management contracts need local coverage and small-business subcontractors to meet subcontracting plan commitments. We can be added as a supplier and perform under your quality program.

Direct service agreement

Commercial & institutional

Walkthrough, written facility scope, price, and a service agreement with terms defined in the proposal. This is where our first documented performance record gets built.
