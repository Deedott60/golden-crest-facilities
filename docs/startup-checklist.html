Two things to know before you start

Most of this is one filing for the business, not two. Every step is tagged — One filing means one of you does it on behalf of the company, and Each of you means both of you have to submit your own information. There are only a few of the second kind.

The boxes are just for tracking. Either of you can tick either box. If you do something together, you both tick. The point is that neither of you has to wonder whether the other already did it.

Do this one first

Book the free APEX Accelerator counselor. It is a phone call, it costs nothing, and it does not depend on anything else on this list. They will check your registrations before you submit them and save you weeks of rework. Everything below goes faster after that call.

    Phase 2

    Federal registration

    Start as early as possible

    This is the government contracting foundation, and it is here rather than at the end for one reason: SAM.gov can take up to ten business days to activate, and the certifications in Phase 3 cannot even be applied for until it is live. Start it the moment the EIN and bank account exist, then carry on with Phase 4 while it processes.

            One filing, or both of you?

            The short answer to the question everyone asks first.

            StepHow it works
            NC Articles of OrganizationOne filing. One form for the company. Both of you are listed as members.
            Operating agreementOne document, both signatures. This is where 50/50 gets recorded.
            EINOne filing. One of you is listed as the "responsible party" — it does not give her more ownership.
            Business bank accountOne account. Go together; both of you sign as signers. Bring both IDs and the filed Articles.
            SAM.govOne registration, but one administrator. SAM allows only one Entity Administrator at a time, tied to that person's own Login.gov account. She registers, then immediately invites the other as a second administrator.
            WOSB / EDWOSBOne application, two sets of personal documents. The application is for the company, but each of you submits your own citizenship and personal financial information.
            Local and state vendor registrationsOne each. One registration per buyer, for the company. Either of you can do them.

            Reference

            What to understand about government work

            Read once. None of this is a reason not to do it — it is why the plan is paced the way it is.

            Where being women-owned actually helps

            It helps a great deal on federal contracts and for getting onto a large contractor's subcontractor list. Those carry real set-asides and participation goals.

            It does not help at Gaston County, the City of Gastonia, or Gaston County Schools. Local buying is mostly lowest responsible bidder, and North Carolina's state program is gender- and race-neutral now. Those are won by being registered, local, reliable and priced right.

            That is why both tracks run at once: the certifications open federal doors, and the local registrations pay the bills while those doors open.

            Some federal cleaning cannot be bid by anyone

            Much federal building cleaning is reserved under the AbilityOne program for nonprofits employing people who are blind or severely disabled. When a building is on that list the agency must buy from the assigned nonprofit — no competition, and no certification changes it.

            Ask the APEX counselor which facilities near us are not covered. That one question saves months.

            Federal work sets your wage the day you hire someone

            Most federal service contracts fall under the Service Contract Act: the Department of Labor sets the minimum wage for janitors in your county, plus a health and welfare benefit of $5.92 per hour as of August 2026 — roughly $12,000 a year on top of wages for one full-time cleaner.

            While it is just the two of you, this matters much less. The moment you hire someone onto a federal job, or a larger contractor hires you onto one of theirs, it applies. Never price that work at your normal commercial rate.

            You get paid slowly

            Government and institutional buyers usually pay 30 to 60 days after invoice. With no employees that is not a payroll crisis, but you could work a month or two before the first check while buying supplies up front. Know the payment terms before signing, and start with a contract you can afford to float.
