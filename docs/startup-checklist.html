Two things to know before you start

Most of this is one filing for the business, not two. Every step is tagged — One filing means one of you does it on behalf of the company, and Each of you means both of you have to submit your own information. There are only a few of the second kind.

The boxes are just for tracking. Either of you can tick either box. If you do something together, you both tick. The point is that neither of you has to wonder whether the other already did it.

This is the formation and registration checklist. Finishing it gives the company its real name, identifiers, registrations, certification applications, links, reminders, and accurate website information. It does not mean the company is ready to accept a cleaning contract. Insurance, equipment, safety, staffing, pricing, and customer agreements will be handled in a separate operating-launch checklist after the registrations are underway.

Do this one first

Book the free NC Government Contracting Assistance Program counselor. It is North Carolina's APEX Accelerator service through the SBTDC. The call costs nothing, does not depend on having every registration finished, and gives you one counselor to check the order and current rules before you submit anything.

Phase 1

Set the company up

1–2 weeks · strict order

The proposed ownership split is 50/50. Put the ownership, responsibilities, contributions, decision rights, and exit rules in writing before filing. Then make the legal name, EIN, bank, address, email, and phone match everywhere.

    Phase 2

    Federal registration

    When legal, EIN, bank, and address records match

    Create separate personal login accounts, register the company for federal awards, verify the registration becomes active, and give both founders authorized access.

      Phase 3

      Local and state registration

      After formation · mostly free

      Register with the closest realistic buyers while building the commercial customer base. These systems have their own rules and are not dependent on an active federal SAM registration.

        Phase 4

        Certifications

        After the required registrations are active

        Apply only for programs that match the actual ownership, control, financial eligibility, location, and business history. The official SBA applications are free.

          Phase 5

          Fill the real information into the website and vendor packet

          As each item is issued

          Replace every placeholder with the exact legal name, identifiers, contacts, service area, and active certification status. Leave anything unissued clearly pending.

            One filing, or both of you?

            The short answer to the question everyone asks first.

            StepHow it works
            NC Articles of OrganizationOne filing. One form creates the company. The operating agreement and company records establish the 50/50 ownership; do not assume the Articles alone prove every ownership and control detail.
            Operating agreementOne document, both signatures. This is where 50/50 gets recorded.
            EINOne filing. One of you is listed as the "responsible party" — it does not give her more ownership.
            Business bank accountOne account. Go together; both of you sign as signers. Bring both IDs and the filed Articles.
            SAM.govOne company registration, separate personal accounts. Each founder uses her own Login.gov account. One starts the entity registration, then invites the other to an administrator role so access is never dependent on one person.
            WOSB / EDWOSBOne application, two sets of personal documents. The application is for the company, but each of you submits your own citizenship and personal financial information.
            Local and state vendor registrationsOne each. One registration per buyer, for the company. Either of you can do them.

            What the codes and numbers mean

            Classification codes describe the work. Identifiers identify the legal company. Certifications prove an approved status. They are not interchangeable.

            TermPlain meaning
            NAICS 561720The primary industry classification for janitorial services. Buyers use NAICS to classify and search for vendors; it is not a certification.
            PSC S201The federal Product Service Code for custodial and janitorial services. It describes what is being purchased; it is not a credential.
            EINThe IRS federal tax identifier for the business. It is free from the IRS and is not the same as a UEI.
            UEIThe Unique Entity ID issued through SAM.gov for federal award systems.
            CAGEA Commercial and Government Entity code assigned through the federal registration process for U.S. entities.
            SAM activeThe federal entity registration has finished processing and is currently active. A submitted registration is not yet active.
            Capability statementA one-page business resume for buyers. It should contain only real services, identifiers, credentials, contacts, and company proof.

            What to understand about government work

            Read once. None of this is a reason not to do it — it is why the plan is paced the way it is.

            Where being women-owned actually helps

            It can matter significantly for eligible federal set-asides and some prime-contractor supplier programs, but certification never guarantees an opportunity, meeting, subcontract, or award.

            Local treatment varies by buyer. North Carolina ended the state HUB office/program and NCSBE is race- and gender-neutral, but local programs and existing certifications may follow separate rules. Confirm each buyer's current policy rather than assuming women-owned status does or does not help.

            That is why both tracks run at once: the certifications open federal doors, and the local registrations pay the bills while those doors open.

            Screen every federal cleaning requirement for AbilityOne

            Some federal custodial work is a mandatory source under AbilityOne. When a specific requirement is on the Procurement List and available under the applicable rules, small-business certification does not override that priority unless an authorized exception applies.

            Ask the counselor how to check each specific facility and requirement before spending time on it.

            Covered federal service work carries contract-specific labor rules

            For covered service contracts above the applicable threshold, the solicitation's current wage determination and clauses govern classifications, base wages, fringe benefits, and related obligations for covered contractor and subcontractor employees. The amount is not one universal number.

            Review the exact wage determination, overtime, leave, and any predecessor agreement before pricing. Do not reuse a commercial rate without that review.
